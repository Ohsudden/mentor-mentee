About us

MMMatch is a platform that helps match mentors with groups of mentees based on shared interests, availability, and goals. It automates the process to make mentorship more efficient and meaningful.

We use smart algorithms to analyze the information you provide in your profile and survey. Based on this, we create diverse groups of mentees and assign mentors whose experience and availability best match the group.

Mentees are grouped into teams of 15–20 people who share similar interests but come from diverse backgrounds, programs, and experiences. This ensures both relevance and diversity in discussions.
